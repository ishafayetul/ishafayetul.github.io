## Project overview
The DIP Tech Lab project explored autonomous ground robotics for disaster-response applications.

## Engineering problem
A response robot needs mapping, navigation and obstacle handling in spaces where routes and terrain may change. LiDAR and IMU measurements provide inputs to the robot’s mapping and navigation software.

## Engineering scope
My research work focused on a modular ROS 2 design for navigation, perception and decision-making, with real-time mapping, obstacle avoidance, and LiDAR and IMU integration.
