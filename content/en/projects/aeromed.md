## Project overview
AeroMed pairs an aerial platform with a ground rover to explore a practical way of providing medical support in remote areas. The project is supported by the ICT Innovation Fund of the Government of Bangladesh.

## Engineering problem
An aerial platform and a ground vehicle have different payload, control and operating constraints. Their handoff depends on a docking and release mechanism and on mechanical and electrical interfaces that work across both platforms.

## My role
I lead the project. The team’s engineering scope includes UAV platform development, drone mission testing, rover docking and release, CAD and electromechanical integration.

## Development scope
The ground rover’s planned control mode is manual teleoperation. The development plan covers UAV mission tests and integration of the rover release mechanism. The project is in development.
