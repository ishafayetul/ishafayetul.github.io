## Project overview
Phoenix was developed by MIST Mongol Barota for the University Rover Challenge. The team’s IEEE Access paper documents the rover as a human-assistant platform.

## Engineering problem
The rover had to bring together a mobile base, a manipulator and a science subsystem. Each subsystem introduced distinct electrical and control interfaces that had to work within the integrated rover.

## My contributions
I served as electrical team lead. My work included circuit design for the rover body, robotic arm and science chamber, and embedded software for the electrical systems. Mechanical design and overall rover development were team efforts.

## Result
MIST Mongol Barota earned the only perfect score in the Equipment Servicing Mission at the 2021 Virtual University Rover Challenge. The linked paper and demonstration document the team’s work.
