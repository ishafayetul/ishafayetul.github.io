## Project overview
IHABOT v2 is an indoor medical assistant robot developed in the Department of Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering at the University of Dhaka. Its software repository refers to the platform as AMAR and describes intended tasks such as carrying medicine and medical supplies.

## Engineering problem
Indoor assistance depends on a robot that can map its surroundings, estimate its pose and plan motion while keeping obstacle information current.

## My contributions
I developed and integrated ROS-based autonomous navigation, LiDAR mapping and sensor fusion. I integrated sensors and microcontrollers for onboard processing and control and worked on navigation testing.

## Technical implementation
The repository documents 2D SLAM and a ROS navigation stack built around odometry, localization and path planning. A global planner provides route guidance and TEB handles local trajectory planning.
